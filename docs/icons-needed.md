# Object images for the videos

Every object a video names or highlights (`{ obj: … }` in `src/narration-scenes/short-0*.js`), and whether it has a picture.
Generated by Claude on 2026-10-21 from the five current videos.

**Two kinds of picture:**

- **Icon** (what shows on the chart and in the videos): `content/icons/<file>.webp`, **128×128 px, WebP, transparent background**, the object centred and filling most of the square, no text, no frame. The existing ones are a mix of real photos cut out on transparent (planets, moons) and AI paintings (stars, black holes, particles): realistic, lit from the upper left, slightly glowing on dark.
- **Photo** (the side panel when you click an object): `content/images/<file>.webp`. Not shown in the videos.

The file name is the slug in the table. **Catalogue dots** (the Titanic, the Eiffel Tower, statues…) are drawn as small dots, not icons: an icon for them needs a small code change to show it, so do the objects first.
