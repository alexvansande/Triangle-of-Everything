NGC 7538 is a star-forming emission nebula about 9,100 light-years away in Cepheus. It is home to the largest known [[Protostar]], NGC 7538 S — a still-forming giant embedded in a collapsing core roughly a third of a parsec across (the scale plotted here), destined to become an O-type star. The surrounding cloud holds enough gas to build entire clusters of massive stars.
