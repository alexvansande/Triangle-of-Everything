The Crab Nebula: the expanding cloud of gas and heavy elements left by a massive star that blew up, seen by Chinese and Japanese astronomers as a new star in 1054 CE and still spreading at about 1,500 km/s. It is about 11 light-years across and holds a few Suns of gas, with the star's crushed core, a pulsar spinning thirty times a second, at its centre. Hubble image, NASA, ESA, J. Hester and A. Loll.
