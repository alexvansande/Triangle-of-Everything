The **Totem Pole** in Monument Valley (on the Navajo Nation, Arizona–Utah border) is a needle of red de Chelly sandstone about **120 m tall** and only a dozen metres wide, the last remnant of a cliff that has otherwise worn away. Climbing it has been banned since the 1970s.

Its mass here is an estimate: a column that size holds about 14,000 m³ of sandstone, roughly **34,000 t**, a few times the Eiffel Tower in a slab you could almost hug.
