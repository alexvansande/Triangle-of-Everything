The same weather-balloon model as **Weather Balloon**, expanded near its nominal burst diameter of **19.8 ft, or about 6 m**, listed for the Kaymont HAB-600.

The estimated **1.4 kg total mass stays the same**: a 600 g envelope, an assumed 500 g instrument-and-rigging load, and the helium from a 1.5 m launch fill. This assumes no gas loss. Its volume becomes about 65 times larger, so its average density falls by the same factor. Actual burst diameter depends on conditions and the individual balloon.

Size refers to the balloon body, not the suspension line.
