R136a1: the heaviest star known, in the R136 cluster of the Tarantula Nebula in the Large Magellanic Cloud. It weighs about 200 Suns (estimates range from about 170 to 300, depending on the models) and is some 40 times the Sun's radius, shining several million times brighter. Stars this heavy live only a few million years and lose mass fast through fierce winds.
