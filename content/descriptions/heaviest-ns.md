**PSR J0740+6620** is one of the heaviest neutron stars known, about 2.08 Suns, and the heaviest whose radius has been measured (NICER: about 12.5 km). The heaviest of all, PSR J0952−0607 at about 2.35 Suns, has no measured radius.

It is near the maximum mass a neutron star can sustain (~2.3 solar masses) before collapsing into a black hole. The densest observable matter in the universe.
