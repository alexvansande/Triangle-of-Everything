**Devils Tower** (Bear Lodge, Mato Tipila) in Wyoming rises **265 m** above the rubble at its foot: a stump of cooled magma, about 300 m across at the base and 90 m at the top, split into giant hexagonal columns as it shrank. The softer rock around it has worn away over some 40 million years. It was the first US national monument (1906), and the landing site in *Close Encounters of the Third Kind*.

Its mass here is an estimate: a tapering column of that size holds about **8.7 million m³** of phonolite porphyry, about **23 million tonnes**.
