The **Sylacauga meteorite** is the only well-documented meteorite to have hit a person. On **30 November 1954**, a **3.86 kg** stone crashed through the roof of a house in Sylacauga, Alabama, bounced off a radio and struck **Ann Hodges** on the hip as she napped on her couch, leaving a huge bruise. Her landlady sued for the stone; Hodges kept it after paying her $500, and it is now in the Alabama Museum of Natural History. A farmer, Julius McKinney, found a second piece (1.59 kg) the next day and sold it, reportedly for enough to buy a car and a house.

It is an **H4 ordinary chondrite**, the most common kind of stony meteorite: a grapefruit-sized piece of a 4.6-billion-year-old asteroid, about 3.4 times as dense as water.
