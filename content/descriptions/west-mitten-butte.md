**West Mitten Butte** is one of the two "Mittens" of Monument Valley, the red sandstone buttes from countless Westerns. Each is a block of **de Chelly sandstone** with a thin "thumb" standing off one side, sitting on a skirt of softer Organ Rock shale; its summit is 261 m above the ground around it.

Here it is counted from where the red cliff rises off the shale slope, the part that clearly stands out: a block about **600 × 250 m and 150 m high**, roughly **54 million t** (an estimate). Counting the slope down to the valley floor as well would make it about four times heavier. Both are the work of erosion: the valley was once a continuous plateau.
