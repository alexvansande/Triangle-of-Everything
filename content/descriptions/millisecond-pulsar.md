**PSR J0437−4715**, 510 light-years away, is the nearest and brightest millisecond pulsar, spinning 174 times a second. Radio timing of its orbit gives its mass, 1.42 Suns, and NICER its radius, about 11.4 km.

A millisecond pulsar is a neutron star spinning hundreds of times per second, spun up by accreting matter from a companion star. Some are more precise timekeepers than atomic clocks.
