Everything that can still reach us. Its edge is the cosmic event horizon, about 16.6 billion light-years away: nothing that happens beyond it now can ever affect us. No mass, no light, no information. Add up all the matter and energy inside, about 10⁵⁶ g, most of it dark energy, and the sphere lands on the black-hole line: a sphere this size, at the average density of the universe, is about as dense as a black hole its size. The match is exact a little further in, at the Hubble radius, and it will be exact for the horizon itself in the far future, when the two meet. It is our universe, not the universe: a galaxy at its edge has its own, centred on itself.
