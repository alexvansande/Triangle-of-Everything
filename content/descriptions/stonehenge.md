**Stonehenge** is a prehistoric stone monument on Salisbury Plain in Wiltshire, England. Its central sarsen circle is about **30 metres across**, with smaller bluestones and taller trilithons within it. The illustration shows the surviving monument, rather than a reconstruction of a complete ring.

The chart uses a rough **1,000-tonne estimate** for the surviving stones as a group, not a measured total. English Heritage gives about 25 tonnes as the average sarsen mass; the lintels and bluestones are smaller. This estimate excludes the surrounding earthworks, soil and outlying Heel Stone. Size refers to the central stone circle, so the plotted object includes the air between its stones.
