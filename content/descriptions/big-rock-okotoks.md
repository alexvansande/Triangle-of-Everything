**Big Rock**, the Okotoks Erratic in Alberta, is a broken boulder of quartzite **41 m long, 18 m wide and 9 m high**, about **16,500 t**. Glaciers carried it hundreds of kilometres from the Rockies some 15,000 years ago and left it on the open prairie, part of a 600 km line of such stones (the Foothills Erratics Train). In Blackfoot stories it is a rock Napi angered, which chased him across the plains until bats broke it in two.
