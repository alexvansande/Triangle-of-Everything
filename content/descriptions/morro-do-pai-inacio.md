**Morro do Pai Inácio** is the flat-topped mesa that is the postcard of the **Chapada Diamantina** in Bahia, Brazil: sheer walls of ancient sandstone and quartzite rising about **300 m** above the plain to a summit at 1,120 m, with a 20-minute climb to the top. Legend says an enslaved man, Inácio, escaped his pursuers by leaping off its edge, holding an umbrella.

Its mass here is a rough estimate from its footprint on maps (no published figure): about 1.2 by 0.6 km at its foot and 300 m high, about **280 million t** of rock.
