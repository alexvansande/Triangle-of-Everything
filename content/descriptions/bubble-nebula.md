The Bubble Nebula (NGC 7635) is an emission nebula about 7,100 light-years away in Cassiopeia. Its almost perfect sphere, roughly 7 light-years across, is blown by the ferocious stellar wind of a single hot [[Massive Star]] — SAO 20575, some 40 times the Sun's mass — which sits visibly off-center inside its own bubble. The wind inflates the shell at over 100,000 km/h into the colder gas around it.
