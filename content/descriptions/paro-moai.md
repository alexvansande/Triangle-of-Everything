**Paro** is the tallest **moai** ever raised on Easter Island (Rapa Nui): about **10 m tall and 82 t**, carved like almost all moai from the soft volcanic tuff of the Rano Raraku quarry and moved, most likely "walked" upright with ropes, about 6 km to its platform. It stood until the late 1700s or 1800s, when, like every moai, it was toppled; it still lies face down.

Even bigger is **El Gigante**, still attached to the rock in the quarry: about 21 m tall and perhaps 145–180 t, so big it could probably never have been moved.
