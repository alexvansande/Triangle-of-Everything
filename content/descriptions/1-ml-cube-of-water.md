A millilitre is a cube one centimetre on each side, and that much water weighs one gram: the metric system was built on it. This chart measures things across, as balls, because most of what it shows is round. Shaped into a ball, the same gram of water is a drop 1.24 cm wide, the [[1 mL of Water]] beside it. On a scale spanning 60 orders of magnitude, that 24% hardly moves it.
