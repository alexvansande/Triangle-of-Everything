Santos-Dumont's **14-bis**, the distinctive box-wing canard aircraft flown publicly in Paris in 1906.

This entry uses the Museu do Ar's listed **12 m wingspan and 160 kg aircraft mass**. The mass is not a loaded takeoff weight. As with the chart's other aircraft, its bulk-density placement is an approximation; the size readout retains the wingspan.
