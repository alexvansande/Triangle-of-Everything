Barnard 68 is a dark molecular cloud — a Bok globule — about 500 light-years away in Ophiuchus. Half a light-year across and only twice the Sun's mass, it is so cold (~16 K) and dense that it swallows the light of every star behind it, appearing as a black hole punched in the star field. It is on the verge of collapse: in a few hundred thousand years, this darkness will ignite into a new star.
