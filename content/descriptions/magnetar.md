**SGR 1806−20**, on the far side of the Milky Way, gave off the brightest flash ever recorded from beyond the Solar System: a giant flare in December 2004 strong enough to disturb Earth's upper atmosphere. No magnetar's mass or radius has been measured, so it is plotted at a typical 1.4 Suns and 12 km.

A magnetar is a neutron star with the strongest magnetic fields in the universe — a quadrillion times stronger than Earth's. A magnetar halfway to the Moon could erase every credit card on Earth.
