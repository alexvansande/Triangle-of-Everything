The **Stone of the Pregnant Woman** (Hajar al-Hibla) is a Roman limestone block still lying in its quarry at **Baalbek**, Lebanon: about **20.5 m long, 4–5 m wide and 4.3 m high, roughly 1,000 t**. It was cut for the temple of Jupiter 900 m away, perhaps to sit beside the Trilithon's three 800 t blocks, but never moved. Two even bigger blocks lie next to it: the Stone of the South (about 1,240 t) and the Forgotten Stone found in 2014 (about 1,650 t), the largest stone blocks anyone has ever quarried.
