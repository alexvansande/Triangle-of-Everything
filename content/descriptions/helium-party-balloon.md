A helium party balloon carries a surprisingly substantial fraction of its mass in the gas inside it.

This is a **representative estimate**, not a particular product: a 30 cm spherical balloon with an assumed 3 g latex skin and about 2.4 g of helium, for **about 5.4 g total**. Ribbon and string are excluded. Helium mass is calculated with the ideal-gas law at 15°C and sea-level pressure. Actual balloon shapes, skin weights and inflation pressures vary.

Its plotted density uses the entire inflated volume, including the gas, rather than the density of rubber.
