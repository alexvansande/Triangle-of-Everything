The **Zeppelin NT** is a helium-filled, semi-rigid airship with an internal framework and swivelling propellers.

For the N07-101, its operator lists **75 m length, 8,425 m³ envelope volume and 8,050 kg maximum takeoff weight**. The chart uses that published aviation weight directly. The specification does not separately identify the lifting-gas mass, so no speculative gas correction has been added.

Bulk-density placement approximates the published mass divided by the inflated envelope volume; the small additional volume of the gondola is omitted. The size readout retains its full length.
