Hopf particles: a prototype

Every curve is a fiber on the 3-sphere S³: a Hopf circle or a torus knot, rotated in four dimensions and stereographically projected into 3D. One grammar maps each particle's quantum numbers onto the fibers. Drag a Hopf view to turn it; "open" shows it full-window.

Spin → ribbon. 0 round tube · ½ Möbius band (one side, one colour) · 1 full twist (two sides)
Charge → winding p/q. p turns around the axis per q around the tube. Neutral = unlinked meridian rings
Quarks → q = 3. Charge in thirds, colour in threes: every quark is 3-fold symmetric
Generation → nested shells. 1, 2 or 3 concentric tori (leptons) or parallel strands (quarks)
Colour charge → R, G, B. Anticolours are cyan, magenta, yellow
Boson ribbon sides. Front: what it is. Back: what it turns things into (same on both sides if it changes nothing)
Antiparticle → mirror image. p → −p, the opposite-handed fibration
Family → texture. Quarks: bold knots · leptons: translucent silk · forces: single bands · Higgs: all of S³
Weak interaction → 5D slice. Neutrinos only flicker into our slice
