### Fact check

- **Right:** the Schwarzschild line as the top-left edge; nothing above or to the left of it; a singularity
  "thought to be"; the 45° line; the Sun as a black hole about 6 km wide, the Earth a marble (~1.8 cm); known
  black holes from collapsing cores or grown by gas and mergers; Sgr A* ~4 million Suns; the supermassive ones
  less dense than water; M87* the first photographed; TON 618's horizon (radius ~800–1,300 AU for 40–66 billion
  Suns) far bigger than Pluto's orbit (~40 AU); the two places (dying stars' cores, the very first instants);
  the four searches; Hawking radiation; the window; galaxies 5–10× heavier than what we see; no new particle
  needed (fixed from rec47's "no new physics"); gravity possibly wrong (modified gravity); the crystal tracks;
  the "cosmic bullet" (Sidhu, Scherrer & Starkman 2019: such lumps would wound like a gunshot, and no such
  injuries are seen); "the very first moments" (fixed from "nanoseconds").
- **Wrong, left out of the cut:** "from a few Suns to billions times the size of our Sun". A black hole of a
  billion Suns has a billion times the Sun's mass but is only ~8,000 times its width. The other try, "a few
  Suns to billions of stars", is used (as heavy as billions of stars; fine).
- **Imprecise, fine:** "is it possible that most matter in the universe is them?" (dark matter is most of the
  *matter*, right).
