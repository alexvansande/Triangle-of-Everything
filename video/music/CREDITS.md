The music is mixed under the voice: lowered, faded in and out, ducked while
the narrator talks, and around the scenes' own sound effects. Its
license allows that ("chop, splice, compress…"); the credit should say the music is
Kevin MacLeod's, and the narration and effects are ours.

On YouTube, a Content ID claim on these tracks usually goes away once the
credit is in the description; dispute it after adding the credit
([incompetech's notes](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/youtube-contentid.html)).
If a claim comes from someone who isn't Kevin MacLeod, write to
help@incompetech.com.

The mp3s aren't in git (`video/music/fetch.sh` downloads them); the shorts'
mixes (`public/narration/short-*/mix.m4a`) include them.
