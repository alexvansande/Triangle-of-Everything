Infinite background: noise vs image warp

A real crop of the z5 background tiles (the sharpest level), around the Hubble radius. Every panel shares one camera: drag to pan, scroll to zoom, or press “Fly in”.
The noise is pinned to chart position. Each octave fades in once the painted pixels are too coarse to show its scale, and fades out once it is finer than a screen pixel, so zooming in never pops.
Click “Solo” on a panel to see it large at 16:9, then press C to hide the controls for a clean capture (Esc to return).
