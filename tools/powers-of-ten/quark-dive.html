Quark dive

The dive from the quarks to the Planck grid as a chain of worlds: each world's centre third holds the next, three times finer, painted with its own rule and coloured by the parent's density, so the shapes resolve into texture instead of fading. When a child fills the frame its clock ramps up from zero and the parent, by then huge, has stopped. Edit the chain below (rules from the dense-automata bench), then Restart.

Each level: fam (smoothlife, lenia, mnca), its rule, speed (steps per frame), accent: the colour its texture paints over whatever colour the ancestors give that point (level 0 paints over base, the space). It only shows inside the ancestors' creatures: inness = [lo, hi] is the smoothstep of a level's value that counts as "inside". density = [where the parent is empty, where it is dense]: the coarse density the clamp pulls the child toward; zero clamp leaves the texture uniform and only the colour follows.
