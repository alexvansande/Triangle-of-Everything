Dense automata

Patterns for the dive inside the quark: cellular automata that fill the plane instead of floating a few gliders on black. Pick a family and a preset, colour it as a world (background, middle, creature), and turn the magnifier up to see what the zoom will find when it dives into one of its shapes. Lenia and MNCA run one rule at every point; Gray-Scott is two coupled fields.
