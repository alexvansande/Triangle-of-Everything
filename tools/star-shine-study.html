A little light on the wall

Circular stellar lights and the Y → T → L brown-dwarf transition.
Hover or tap a sample: the star comes forward, its light spreads and dims.

Black backing discs stay aligned as the stars grow. Light spreads and dims on hover. Y brown dwarfs keep the strongest shadow; T and L have progressively weaker shadows and larger warm auras. Stellar halos increase with chart mass, with a gentle upper limit. The chart uses the same light profiles.
